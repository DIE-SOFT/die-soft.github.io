You're coming back to a dream you haven't had in some time, Nemo. It must feel familiar, but something is wrong…

Little Nemo and the Guardians of Slumberland combines metroidvania exploration with classic 2D platformer gameplay. You'll discover new toys throughout Slumberland, each granting a unique ability that will expand your platforming toolkit, opening up new areas to explore and new strategies for defeating enemies and bosses. Slumberland is full of wildly varied regions, each with its own atmosphere, inhabitants, and surprises, all interconnected into a vast, seamless world packed with secrets and collectibles for the curious dreamer to find.

The waters of Oblivion are consuming Slumberland. It's up to you, Nemo. To save it, you'll need to remember this dream before it's washed away by the dawn and lost forever.

Little Nemo is available NOW on Steam! as well as itch.io and will be coming to Nintendo Switch later this year (stay tuned for more announcement about timing).

Key Features

  • An original soundtrack composed entirely by Peter Berkman of Anamanaguchi.
  • A world full of hidden secrets, collectibles, and a colorful cast of characters.
  • Challenging bosses that can be defeated by mastering the toys you've collected.
  • A unique Rank Up system for players who love a challenge.
  • Customizable difficulty options for players who prefer to focus on the adventure.
  • Finish in roughly 10 - 15 hours, or spend even more time to fully explore and find everything!
